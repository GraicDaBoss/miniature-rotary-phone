# AA26-Assignment


# My proposal for this assignment is to make an alien amphibian swimming in an underwater abyss. I want it to interact with bells and symbols that make harmonic sounds.
# I am inspired by a game called Harold Halibut that has alot of minor interactive features in the alien map. Its quite unique and inhuman and holographic. I want the alien creature to fiend symbols that match harmonically, almost like a scale. I will also implement minor obstacles to showcase its movement and procedural animations. As sound increases, Id like the creature to look like its dancing or listening the music. Id like the creature to interact with the bells as if they are collectibles, and take time between bells to 'enjoy' the music. I  want it to be percived as a smart instrument that plays its own song differently every time you press play. This will involve me programming harmonic steering procedures for the creature and a chord system that allows bells only to be collected in harmony with other appropriate chords.
